How I work

My usual route from "Hmm…" to "Okay, let's build it."

  1. 01

    Observe

    Understand the people, context, market and problem.

  2. 02

    Understand

    Research, analyse, question assumptions and frame the actual problem.

  3. 03

    Build

    Turn the insight into a product, process, system or decision.

  4. 04

    Improve

    Measure what happened, learn and iterate.

I don't always follow this order perfectly. Real projects rarely cooperate.
